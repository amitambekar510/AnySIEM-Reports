# SLA and Quarterly Service Review: Section Blueprint

Blueprint for a managed-service quarterly review. Respect the user's disclosure level for SLA figures.

## Section index

| ID | Section | Include |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | Cover and Document Control | Always |
| S1 | Executive Summary | Always |
| S2 | Service Scope and Changes | Always |
| S3 | SLA Performance | Always |
| S4 | Service Metrics | Always |
| S5 | Improvements Delivered | Always |
| S6 | Issues, Risks and Dependencies | Always |
| S7 | Feedback and Satisfaction | When feedback or a survey exists |
| S8 | Roadmap and Next Quarter Plan | Always |
| S9 | Change Requests and Commercial Notes | When change requests exist |
| S10 | Sign-Off and Acknowledgement | Always |

## S0: Cover and Document Control

**Purpose:** Identify the review.

**Content blocks**

- Cover
- Document control table

**Inputs from the user**

- Quarter
- Roles

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001

## S1: Executive Summary

**Purpose:** Summarize service performance and outlook.

**Content blocks**

- Quarter at a glance
- Headline results
- Key issues and decisions needed

**Inputs from the user**

- Disclosure level

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Alert and incident totals
- SLA results

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, RBI IT/Cyber, SEBI CSCRF 2024

## S2: Service Scope and Changes

**Purpose:** Confirm what was delivered and what changed.

**Content blocks**

- Services in scope
- Onboarded and decommissioned sources
- Scope or contract changes

**Inputs from the user**

- Scope and change record

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Source count changes

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, CERT-In 2022

## S3: SLA Performance

**Purpose:** Report each SLA with context.

**Content blocks**

- Table: metric, target, monthly results, quarter result
- Breaches with cause and corrective action
- Trend

**Inputs from the user**

- SLA definitions
- Results, or generic wording in diplomatic mode

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Acknowledgement and response timestamps
- Availability figures

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, RBI IT/Cyber, SEBI CSCRF 2024

**Writing note:** State responsibility for causes honestly, including client-side dependencies.

## S4: Service Metrics

**Purpose:** Show service activity.

**Content blocks**

- Alerts, incidents, hunts, advisories
- Log source health summary

**Inputs from the user**

- Which measures to show

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Counts by type and severity
- Source health summary

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, CERT-In 2022

## S5: Improvements Delivered

**Purpose:** Show value added.

**Content blocks**

- Detections added or tuned
- Automation delivered
- Process improvements

**Inputs from the user**

- Improvements made

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Rule change record

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, MITRE ATT&CK

## S6: Issues, Risks and Dependencies

**Purpose:** Surface what slows the service.

**Content blocks**

- Issue and risk table (item, impact, owner, date)
- Client-side dependencies

**Inputs from the user**

- Open issues

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Coverage gaps

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, SEBI CSCRF 2024

## S7: Feedback and Satisfaction

**Purpose:** Capture the client's view.

**Include when:** When feedback or a survey exists

**Content blocks**

- Feedback received
- Actions taken

**Inputs from the user**

- Feedback

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001

## S8: Roadmap and Next Quarter Plan

**Purpose:** Show the plan.

**Content blocks**

- Planned work, dates, owners
- Decisions needed

**Inputs from the user**

- Plan

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001

## S9: Change Requests and Commercial Notes

**Purpose:** Record scope changes.

**Include when:** When change requests exist

**Content blocks**

- Open change requests
- Notes on scope or commercial impact

**Inputs from the user**

- Open requests

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001

## S10: Sign-Off and Acknowledgement

**Purpose:** Record review.

**Content blocks**

- Team-level sign-off
- Disclaimer

**Inputs from the user**

- Roles

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001
