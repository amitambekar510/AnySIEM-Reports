# SLA and Quarterly Service Review: Questions

Ask in batches. Skip anything already answered or already known from the SIEM profile.

## Batch 1: Context

1. What is the organization or client name, and who will read this report (leadership, client, auditor, regulator)?
2. Is the SOC in-house, provided by a managed service provider, or hybrid? What are the operating hours?
3. What reporting period and audit period does this report cover?
4. What classification label and document ID format should the report use?
5. Template mode (placeholders) or populated mode (filled values)? Exact numbers or diplomatic wording?

## Batch 2: Service

1. What services are contracted?
2. What changed this quarter (sources, scope, people)?

## Batch 3: SLAs

1. What are the SLA metrics and targets?
2. What were the results by month?
3. Were there breaches? What caused them?

## Batch 4: Value

1. What improvements did you deliver?
2. What are the main issues or client-side dependencies?

## Batch 5: Plan

1. What is planned for next quarter?
2. Are there open change requests or feedback to include?
3. Is there anything else you want in this report (a dashboard summary, a custom table, a regulator-specific appendix)?
4. Which roles should sign off? Should the watermark and footer attribution stay or be removed?
