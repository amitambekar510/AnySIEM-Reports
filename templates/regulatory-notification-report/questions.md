# Regulatory Notification Report: Questions

Ask in batches. Skip anything already answered or already known from the SIEM profile.

## Batch 1: Context

1. What is the organization or client name, and who will read this report (leadership, client, auditor, regulator)?
2. Is the SOC in-house, provided by a managed service provider, or hybrid? What are the operating hours?
3. What reporting period and audit period does this report cover?
4. What classification label and document ID format should the report use?
5. Template mode (placeholders) or populated mode (filled values)? Exact numbers or diplomatic wording?

## Batch 2: Reportability

1. Which incident does this relate to, and when was it noticed?
2. Who decided on reportability, and what was decided?
3. Which regulators or parties were involved?

## Batch 3: Submission

1. When, how, and under what reference was it submitted?
2. Are follow-up reports pending?

## Final batch: Close

1. Is there anything else you want in this report (a dashboard summary, a custom table, a regulator-specific appendix)?
2. Which roles should sign off? Should the watermark and footer attribution stay or be removed?
