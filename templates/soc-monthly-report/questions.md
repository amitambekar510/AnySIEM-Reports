# SOC Operations Report: Questions

Ask in batches. Skip anything already answered or already known from the SIEM profile.

## Batch 1: Context

1. What is the organization or client name, and who will read this report (leadership, client, auditor, regulator)?
2. Is the SOC in-house, provided by a managed service provider, or hybrid? What are the operating hours?
3. What reporting period and audit period does this report cover?
4. What classification label and document ID format should the report use?
5. Template mode (placeholders) or populated mode (filled values)? Exact numbers or diplomatic wording?

## Batch 2: Scope

1. Which log source categories are monitored, and roughly how many?
2. Which services are in scope and out of scope (for example vulnerability management)?
3. Which environments or locations are covered?

## Batch 3: Metrics and SLAs

1. How do you define and measure detection time and response time?
2. What is your severity scheme?
3. What are your SLA metrics and targets, and do you want exact results or generic wording?

## Batch 4: Frameworks

1. Which regulations and standards apply to you? (see the framework list in the compliance library)
2. Do you need to show evidence for an audit (for example an annual technology audit)? Which auditor or regulator is the audience?
3. Are there mandatory reporting windows or log retention periods you must evidence?

## Batch 5: Platform data

1. Which dashboards or exports can you share for ingestion, health, alerts, and ATT&CK coverage?
2. What are your retention tiers?
3. Which log sources currently have gaps?

## Batch 6: Close

1. Is there anything else you want in this report (a dashboard summary, a custom table, a regulator-specific appendix)?
2. Which roles should sign off? Should the watermark and footer attribution stay or be removed?
