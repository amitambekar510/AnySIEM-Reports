# Executive / CISO Brief: Section Blueprint

Blueprint for a short leadership brief. Keep it to one or two pages, in plain business language.

## Section index

| ID | Section | Include |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Cover and Distribution | Always |
| E1 | Security Posture at a Glance | Always |
| E2 | Key Events and Incidents | Always |
| E3 | Threat Landscape Relevant to the Business | Always |
| E4 | Risk and Compliance Position | Always |
| E5 | Decisions and Support Needed | Always |
| E6 | Next Period Priorities | Always |

## E0: Cover and Distribution

**Purpose:** Identify period, audience, and classification on one compact cover block.

**Content blocks**

- Title, period, classification
- Distribution list (roles)

**Inputs from the user**

- Audience roles
- Classification

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001

## E1: Security Posture at a Glance

**Purpose:** Give leadership the overall position in under a minute.

**Content blocks**

- Overall status (red, amber, green) with one-line reason
- Four to six headline measures with trend arrows
- One-sentence verdict

**Inputs from the user**

- Status scale definition
- Which measures leadership cares about

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Incident counts by severity
- Detection and response timing
- Platform availability
- Coverage of critical assets

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, SEBI CSCRF 2024, NIST CSF

**Writing note:** Keep it to one screen. Define the red, amber, green scale once.

## E2: Key Events and Incidents

**Purpose:** Describe what mattered, in business terms.

**Content blocks**

- Top events (what happened, business impact, status)
- Notifications made to regulators or customers (if any)

**Inputs from the user**

- Events leadership should know about
- Business impact statements

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Most significant incidents of the period
- Closure status

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** CERT-In 2022, SEBI CSCRF 2024, RBI IT/Cyber, DPDP Act 2023, NPCI/UPI

**Writing note:** Use plain language. Avoid technical identifiers unless asked.

## E3: Threat Landscape Relevant to the Business

**Purpose:** Connect external threats to this organization.

**Content blocks**

- Two or three threat themes relevant to the sector
- What they could mean for the business
- What the SOC is doing about them

**Inputs from the user**

- Sector and crown-jewel systems

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Attack types observed against the organization
- Notable threat intelligence matches

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** MITRE ATT&CK, NIST CSF, CERT-In 2022

## E4: Risk and Compliance Position

**Purpose:** Show regulatory and audit standing without overclaiming.

**Content blocks**

- Frameworks in scope and current evidence status
- Open audit or compliance items
- Key risks and their owners
- AI system security position (when AI systems are in scope)

**Inputs from the user**

- Selected frameworks
- Open items from compliance team

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Evidence readiness indicators

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** CERT-In 2022, SEBI CSCRF 2024, RBI IT/Cyber, DPDP Act 2023, ISO/IEC 27001, NSE/BSE Tech Audit, NPCI/UPI, ISO/IEC 27090, ISO/IEC 42001

**Writing note:** Do not write 'compliant' without evidence and compliance-team agreement.

## E5: Decisions and Support Needed

**Purpose:** State clearly what the SOC needs from leadership.

**Content blocks**

- Numbered asks (decision, reason, deadline)

**Inputs from the user**

- Budget, headcount, access, or policy requests

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001, SEBI CSCRF 2024

## E6: Next Period Priorities

**Purpose:** Show the plan.

**Content blocks**

- Three to five priorities with owners

**Inputs from the user**

- Planned work

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001
