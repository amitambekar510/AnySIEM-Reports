# AI Management System Review Report: Section Blueprint

Blueprint for reporting on an AI management system. Built around the management system cycle of ISO/IEC 42001 (published December 2023). Use only the user's own inputs. Do not state certification status unless the user provides the certificate details. Do not reproduce text from the standard.

## Section index

| ID | Section | Include |
|---|---|---|
| AM0 | Cover and Document Control | Always |

| AM2 | Leadership and Policy | Always |

## AM0: Cover and Document Control

**Purpose:** Identify the document, period, and owner.

**Content blocks**

- Cover with title, period, and classification
- Document control table

**Inputs from the user**

- Document ID format
- Author, reviewer, approver roles

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 27001

## AM1: Scope and Context

**Purpose:** Define what the AI management system covers.

**Content blocks**

- AI management system scope and boundaries
- Organization's role (develops, provides, or uses AI systems)
- Interested parties and their requirements (laws, contracts, customers)

**Inputs from the user**

- Scope
- Roles
- Applicable requirements

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- None

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001, DPDP Act 2023, NIST CSF

## AM3: AI Risk and Impact Assessment

**Purpose:** Show how AI risks and impacts are assessed and treated.

**Content blocks**

- Risk assessment method
- Risk summary and treatment plans
- Impact assessment on individuals, groups, and society as the organization defines it
- Fairness, transparency, and bias considerations

**Inputs from the user**

- Risk method and results
- Impact assessments performed

**Data from the SIEM (vendor-neutral)**

- Security risks from monitoring of AI systems

**Compliance hooks (indicative, verify before citing):** ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27090, ISO/IEC 27001, DPDP Act 2023, NIST CSF

**Writing note:** Use the organization's own method and results. Do not invent assessments.
